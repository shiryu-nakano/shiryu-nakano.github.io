Singular Learning Theory (SLT)

What is SLT? Singular Learning Theory (SLT) is a mathematical framework developed by Sumio Watanabe for analyzing statistical learning when the model is singular (non-regular). Key Concepts RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold): A key quantity that determines generalization error Free Energy: Measures model complexity in Bayesian inference Singular Models: Models where the Fisher information matrix is degenerate References Watanabe, S. (2009). Algebraic Geometry and Statistical Learning Theory Watanabe, S. (2018). Mathematical Theory of Bayesian Statistics
